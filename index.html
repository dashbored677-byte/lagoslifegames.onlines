The Six Need Systems & Decay Rates

To survive in Lagos Life, you must continuously manage 6 core personal needs. Letting any reach 0% causes collapse or performance penalties.

1. Bladder Need

Fastest Decay: -7.0% / hr

Requires frequent rest stop visits or home bathrooms. High priority during travel and right before starting shift work.

2. Hunger Need

Decay Rate: -5.5% / hr

Replenish by eating at street stalls like Amala Shitta, local mama put, or cooking meals inside rented apartments.

3. Fun Need

Decay Rate: -4.5% / hr

Maintained by visiting Elegushi Beach, Freedom Park concerts, Quilox Nightclub, or playing mobile games on your phone.

4. Energy Need

Decay Rate: -4.0% / hr

Restored by sleeping in rented housing beds or resting on public benches. Low energy severely penalties work shift output.

5. Hygiene Need

Decay Rate: -3.5% / hr

Shower at home or bucket bath in Face-Me-I-Face-You rentals. Cleanliness determines acceptance at corporate jobs like Finance.

6. Social Need

Decay Rate: -3.0% / hr

Maintained by chatting with NPCs, street vendors, co-workers, and attending Owambe weekend parties across Lekki.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my progress in Lagos Life?

Lagos Life saves your progress automatically in your browser's local storage. To prevent data loss when clearing browser cache, register an email account in the game menu.

What happens if my weekly rent is unpaid?

Failing to pay weekly rent by Sunday 23:59 results in immediate eviction. Your personal items are moved to temporary storage and you spawn at the nearest Danfo park with low hygiene.

10 Pro Lagos Life Survival Tips & Hacks

Tested strategies to optimize money, time, and needs management.

PRO TIP #01

Pre-Shift Need Reset

Always top up Hunger to 100% and Bladder to 100% right before entering work. This triggers the +25% perfect shift cash multiplier.

PRO TIP #02

Toilet Before Transit

Bladder decays at 7.0%/hr (fastest in-game). Using the toilet right before getting on a Danfo prevents unexpected accidents in traffic.

PRO TIP #03

Amala Shitta Budget Meals

Amala Shitta in Surulere provides +45 Hunger for only ₦800, making it 3x more cost-effective than Lekki restaurants.

PRO TIP #04

Danfo vs Uber Commuting

Use cheap Danfo buses (₦300) when energy is above 60%. Use Uber (₦2,500) only when rushing to a high-paying Finance shift with low time left.

PRO TIP #05

Yaba Strategic Hub

Renting in Yaba puts you within walking distance of CcHub (Tech job) and cheap food, saving thousands in weekly transit fees.

PRO TIP #06

Passive Social Gain

Talking to street vendors at Balogun Market passively refills your Social stat while shopping for cheap clothes.

First 10 Minutes & 7-Day Survival Guide

Step-by-step beginner checklist to avoid early eviction and debt.

1

First 10 Minutes: Setup

Select either the Tech Bro trait (for CcHub tech speed) or Street Hustler trait. Check starting funds. If Lapo background, immediately take a Danfo to Mushin or Yaba.

2

First Day: Securing Housing & Job

Rent the Mushin Face-Me-I-Face-You (₦2,400/wk) or Yaba Self-Contain (₦6,000/wk). Travel to CcHub in Yaba or Balogun Market. Apply for an entry-level shift immediately.

3

First Week: Managing Needs & Rent

Complete 5 full shifts during the week. Keep a emergency cash float of at least ₦10,000 to cover Sunday night rent payment automatically.

Top 5 Fatal Beginner Mistakes

  • Going to work dirty or hungry: Reduces shift cash yield by 40%.
  • Taking Uber everywhere early on: Drains starter cash within 3 in-game days.
  • Forgetting Sunday rent deadline: Causes immediate eviction and loss of inventory.
  • Ignoring Bladder decay: Bladder decays fastest (-7.0%/hr). Always check before travel.
  • Buying expensive designer clothes before a car or apartment: Wastes critical early capital.
